## A Market Growing at Unprecedented Scale

The urgency becomes clear when we look at the numbers.

According to industry reports and market research data, the product photography market has grown significantly over the last five years.

In **2021**, the global product photography services market was valued at approximately **$8.2 billion**.

By **2022**, the market had grown to **$9.5 billion**, driven by the explosive growth of e-commerce during and after the pandemic.

The trend accelerated in **2023**, reaching **$11.2 billion**, as more businesses moved online and competition for visual attention intensified.

In **2024**, the market crossed **$13.1 billion**, with AI-powered solutions beginning to emerge but remaining expensive and limited.

By **2025**, the market reached **$15.8 billion**, yet most small businesses still couldn't access affordable, high-quality product photography.

In just four years:

📈 Market size increased by **93%**

📈 Average cost per product shoot increased by **40%**

📈 Demand from small businesses increased by **300%**

Additional market data shows:
* Over **24 million active e-commerce sellers** globally
* Average of **50-100 products** per seller
* Only **15% of small businesses** can afford professional photography
* **85% of sellers** use DIY or low-quality photos

These numbers reveal two realities:
1. Professional product photography is now essential for success.
2. Most sellers can't afford it.
